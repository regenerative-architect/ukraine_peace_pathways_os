# Conflict-to-Regeneration — independent review protocol

Version: 4.1.0 | 16 October 2026 | Foster + Navi / Planetary Restoration Archive

## Why the module exists

The 14 alternatives offer a structured comparison of ways conflict-related financing ecosystems might contribute to civilian recovery without assuming illegitimate legal powers, equivalence between aggressor and victim, or automatic peace dividends. The options are independent research hypotheses, not grants, endorsements, bargains or sanctions waivers.

## Three gating lanes

1. **Independent civilian action today:** public grants, municipal recovery, health, water, land-release-after-accreditation and voluntary civilian R&D. Protect existing legitimate defensive appropriations from automatic conversion.
2. **Conditional reciprocal programs:** only after documented, competent consent, applicable sanctions and asset law, agreed evidence standards and independent verification. Humanitarian law cannot be made conditional on reciprocal concessions.
3. **Post-agreement cooperation:** cross-party environmental restoration, voluntary workforce conversion and reparations-linked financing after legitimate agreement and rights-preserving legal instruments.

## Proposed review procedure

- Confirm the source and as-of date; resolve disputed claims through primary institutions.
- For every actor distinguish capacity, willingness, legal authorization and funding evidence.
- Compare existing appropriations, commitments, actual disbursements and speculative funds without double counting.
- Obtain Ukrainian and affected-community authorization for projects; exclude confidential or sensitive geospatial information.
- Define the counterfactual, civilian benefit measure, denominators, baseline and independent evaluator.
- Review sanctions, dual-use controls, procurement conflicts, data protection, accessibility and ecological harm.
- Publish objections and rejected pathways as well as favorable findings. Do not imply independent review has occurred when it has not.

## Zero-Harm / Anti-Inversion

Restrict publication to safe, aggregate and independently inspectable information. Never facilitate prohibited funds movement, tactical identification, intimidation of financiers, or coercive disarmament.
