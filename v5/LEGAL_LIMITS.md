# Law, implementation and non-enforcement constraints

**Reference snapshot: 16 October 2026.** This is a civilian-policy analytical framing, not a legal opinion, treaty draft accepted by any principal or authority to negotiate. Consult independent Ukrainian constitutional scholars, IHL experts, OSCE/UN/IAEA counsel and relevant competent officials before any external use.

## Security and civilian safeguards (Track 1)

**Mandate versus ability.** The OSCE SMM to Ukraine ceased operations in 2022 after its mandate was not extended. Electronic monitoring, telemetry and sensor grids would require a new legally competent monitor, defined consent/access, incident evidence protocol and protection from repurposing into intelligence or targeting. Drone evidence is restricted to lawfully acquired, delayed, non-sensitive metadata, with no live tactical maps. The 30 km heavy-artillery separation is a proposed bargaining **parameter**; the OS cannot determine whether it is militarily feasible or binding without expert legal and operational review and signatures. [OSCE closed mission](https://www.osce.org/chairpersonship/516933)

**Protected persons.** Wounded/seriously sick POW repatriation and civilian detainee releases engage different provisions, classification, medical review, voluntary rights and ICRC/humanitarian involvement. Compliance cannot be conditioned on trading away mandatory protections. No individual medical/detention records belong in this public OS. [ICRC](https://www.icrc.org/en/law-and-policy/protected-persons-prisoners-war-and-detainees)

**Nuclear safety.** IAEA's Five Principles and Seven Pillars inform assessments and facilitation of repair arrangements. IAEA cannot impose a military exclusion zone or guarantee “absolute” safety. A site-protection agreement is conditional on restraint by competent parties and separate acceptance of monitoring and safe access. Emergency power and cooling are prioritized over impressive diagrams. [IAEA](https://www.iaea.org/topics/response/nuclear-safety-security-and-safeguards-in-ukraine)

**Maritime corridors.** The earlier UN Black Sea Grain Initiative expired in July 2023. AIS is incomplete, spoofable and potentially sensitive; retrospective aggregate arrival/incident indicators do not establish safe passage. Naval and trade routes, vessel identities, insurance, shipping law and port safety cannot be substituted with autonomous algorithms. [IMO](https://www.imo.org/en/mediacentre/hottopics/pages/maritimesecurityandsafetyintheblackseaandseaofazov.aspx)

## Lawful governance and community rights (Track 2)

**Sovereignty and territorial status.** The UN General Assembly rejected the claimed legality of Russia's 2022 purported annexations. A nominal five-year cooling period is not a source of authority to hold annexation-legalizing territorial referenda. Any legally permissible consultation must pass Ukrainian constitutional review, international law, an end to coercion/occupation, electoral safeguards and real displaced-person participation; the software contains no automatic countdown, election trigger or recognition. [UN](https://press.un.org/en/2022/ga12458.doc.htm)

**UN administration is not presumed.** UNTAET in East Timor was expressly authorized under UN Security Council Resolution 1272 in 1999, in its unique historical circumstances. A proposed UN-run zone in Ukraine would require a distinct authority and political feasibility; a Russian Security Council veto and Ukrainian sovereignty are essential issues to assess. Study lawful Ukrainian-administered special service/economic mechanisms as a separate, potentially less intrusive option. [UN SC 1272](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/291410?ln=en)

**Human rights remedies.** European Convention rights and applicable national courts may be relevant, but the ECHR retains jurisdiction regarding Russian conduct only up to the date Russia ceased to be a Convention party (16 September 2022). The ECHR should not be branded a guarantor of future Russian compliance. Ukrainian domestic and other competent monitoring avenues require their own analysis. [ECHR](https://www.echr.coe.int/en/respondent-governments)

## Civilian recovery (Track 3)

**Asset yields.** Do not equate immobilized Russian sovereign principal with freely available cash. As of the 2026 legal baseline, EU extraordinary revenues have standing allocations: 95% to the Ukraine Loan Cooperation Mechanism and 5% to the European Peace Facility. Any new trust requires fresh competent authorization, a non-duplicative funding source, due-process/sovereign-immunity review and external audits. [European Council](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/ukraine-solidarity-financial-support/)

**Green grid.** Ukraine and Moldova synchronized with continental Europe's electricity system in March 2022. Further integration is grid reliability, cross-border trading, safe capacity, climate resilience and operator coordination; the project must not claim to invent or initiate existing links. [ENTSO-E](https://www.entsoe.eu/news/2024/03/15/two-years-since-ukraine-and-moldova-synchronised-electricity-grids-with-eu/)

**Mine action.** AI-based survey is a decision aid only. National mine-action bodies and accredited teams remain responsible for clearance, quality assurance, risk education and certified handover. A zero-miss algorithm is not feasible to assert, and no AI confidence output may be read as “safe to farm.” [IMAS 07.11](https://www.mineactionstandards.org/standards/07-11/)

## Five acceptance gates

1. **Legality:** A qualified expert identifies applicable law, competent authority, rights, objections, and no coerced territorial recognition.
2. **Consent:** Each required principal, facilitator, monitor and third-party guarantor separately accepts a documented role. A source quote alone cannot establish this.
3. **Strategic rationality:** Real parties may reject the deal; alternative-to-agreement, domestic veto and credibility are explicitly evaluated without fabricated payoff scores.
4. **Verify and respond:** Defined, privacy-safe observables, monitor access, contradictory evidence, appeals, denials, breach review and a credible non-escalatory fallback.
5. **Delivery and safety:** Funding, community inclusion, procurement audit, reviewable indicators and immediate protective stop criteria.

**Zero-harm / anti-inversion:** This project publishes high-level civilian and institutional analysis only. No sensitive coordinates, live ISR feeds, identity registers, automated diplomatic outreach, autonomous demining certification or guaranteed compliance labels. These restrictions reduce known misuse pathways, but they are not proof of impossible misuse.
