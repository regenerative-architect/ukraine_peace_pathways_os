# Legal & Institutional Documentation Atlas — Reviewer Guide

**Edition:** v4.2, 16 October 2026  
**Status:** Public research directory; not legal advice, official law, legal clearance, a sanctions authorization, or an exhaustive corpus.

## Scope

The catalog currently covers **28 jurisdictions** (national, EU/supranational, and relevant international institutions) through **59 curated official-publisher links**, including legislation repositories, selected binding legal instruments, treaty registries, case-law search systems, and regulator guidance. Jurisdiction grouping is by the *issuer's* legal authority, not a determination that an instrument governs any given territory, actor, conflict phase, or transaction.

The application works offline for titles, source notes and research tags, but its **linked legal full texts are not stored offline**. Actual linked pages, amendments, treaty status and sanctions licenses require a live connection and renewed review.

## What each source record means

- **Instrument:** entry point for an identified constitution, regulation, statute, treaty or similar text. Inclusion is *not* a finding it is currently in force, authoritative in translation, or applicable to a case.
- **Repository:** government/IGO access point to a catalog or officially operated legal publication portal. Some consolidated *reference* texts have no independent legal effect; locate the original gazette publication when required.
- **Guidance:** the relevant body's explanation of processes or sanctions/licensing. Distinguish it from enforceable law.
- **Jurisdiction:** country/institution from which the source originates. Cross-border sanctions, human-rights agreements and conflict law can overlap; specialist analysis is required.
- **Change risk / reviewed date:** prompts for diligence, **not** a continuously verified feed or guarantee that the URL or law remains current.
- **Legal status:** recorded as unverified pending independent counsel review. Linking a primary publisher is necessary but not sufficient for establishing binding effect.

## Review workflow for a proposed pathway

1. Define the proposed activity, relevant persons/institutions, places and dates. Identify whose authorization is necessary (Ukraine, other sovereign states, UN/OSCE where relevant, technical agencies, competent regulators).
2. Filter the directory by affected jurisdiction and subject. Open each publisher link using a trusted browser and record the access date.
3. Identify the **exact** law, regulation, section, consolidated version, gazette publication, effective date, derogations and amendments. Obtain an authoritative-language text or reviewed translation if needed.
4. Separately check treaty **ratification**, reservations, jurisdiction, applicable territorial scope and court competence.
5. For assets/sanctions, check **current** designation lists, restrictions, general/specific licenses, national transposition and competent authorities. Do not treat asset freezes as funds available for transfer.
6. Record verifiable citations with law identifiers, article or section, publication date, version, source URL, retrieval date, reviewer, and unresolved conflicts. Do not upload confidential casework to unverified public collaboration rooms.
7. Create a Pathway Forge legal-applicability review, seek legal and humanitarian expert sign-off, then update a claim only after the responsible authority approves.

## Limitations and safeguarding

No legal instrument cited by this directory is represented as approved by the parties to the conflict; no authority is granted by software. Use publicly accessible, non-operational research materials. Do not store sensitive civilian locations, intelligence collection methods, private detainee details, military positions or classified data. Avoid speculative assertions that amendments, sanctions waivers, reparations or international administrations have been authorized. Consult competent counsel for actual legal decisions. **No offline copy or SHA-256 digest can prove that a remote source remains current or legally effective.**
