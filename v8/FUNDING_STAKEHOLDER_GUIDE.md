# Funding and support evidence — reviewer guide

**Foster + Navi / Planetary Restoration Archive · Snapshot: 16 October 2026.** Independent civilian policy research; not legal or financial advice; no actor has endorsed this application.

## Scope

`data/funding-atlas.json` links 30 public funding/support records to 78 actor profiles and 22 dated public evidence sources. It is a selected set of salient funding channels, **not a comprehensive global register of every donor, contractor, bank, transaction or government budget line**.

## Classification

1. `budget-aggression` — Russian budget and defence industrial funding in the invasion.
2. `external-material-to-russia` — reported cross-border military equipment transfers to Russia; NOT necessarily cash grants or current deliveries.
3. `defence-ukraine` — lawful self-defence assistance provided to Ukraine, distinct from the aggressor's war financing.
4. `civilian-ukraine` — humanitarian support, civilian reconstruction and macro-finance.
5. `intermediation` — multilaterals and loan, trust-fund, custodial or sovereign-asset proceeds mechanisms; the administrator need not be the original donor.
6. `indirect-exposure` — commodity revenue or potential trade-tax transmission; not proof an individual buyer intended to finance an invasion or violated sanctions.
7. `prospective` — hypothetical future partners and permitted solutions; no actual pledge implied.

Confidence statuses: `confirmed-recurring` (dated, published multi-year or recurrent program), `confirmed-dated` (historical or time-bound public record), `indirect-documented` (aggregate fiscal nexus without traced individual payments), `potential-unverified` (no substantiated commitment). The word **confirmed** applies solely to that specific public financial or materiel relationship, never to adoption of PRA or agreement with its analysis.

## Measurements must never be summed blindly

**Allocation** is budgeted or authorized resources, not a delivered payment. **Commitment** may be forward-looking. **Disbursement** is a transfer documented as paid. **Loan** is repayable. **Guarantee** is contingent exposure. **SIPRI TIV** tracks arms transfers, not dollars. **Revenue**, including Russian oil/gas taxes, is fungible and does not trace private proceeds to an individual military purchase. **World Bank mobilized** resources often consist of third-party donor contributions, overlapping figures at different implementation layers. **EU, G7 and bilateral totals** may overlap. The application's source notes deliberately retain original units, dates and limitations.

## Required diligence before publication or outreach

- Open original source; establish publishing date, exact claim, currency, fiscal year, authorization stage and whether the statement has been corrected.
- Where legal consequences are asserted, consult official statutes and applicable live sanctions databases; the finance dataset is not an OFAC/EU/UK compliance screening list.
- Attribute institutional conduct accurately; neither a government, organization nor an individual should be described as an illegal war funder based solely on ordinary trade exposure.
- Do not identify private individuals or confidential transfers and do not connect this application to surveillance, covert transactions or battlefield systems.
- Any cooperation alternative must be voluntary, Ukraine-consented where required, transparent, lawful, independently monitored and reversible after serious violations.
- Present inferred opportunities only as proposals; never assume governmental willingness or future payments.

## Access and use

- Open `index.html` and navigate to **Financing & support evidence** or **Stakeholder atlas**.
- Filter by funding relationship and evidence status; select an entry to open its independently scrolling dossier; use `Export reviewed-source dossier` or CSV export.
- Select **Assess alternative in Pathway Forge** to prefill a nine-stage evaluation question. This is an analyst-only handoff; no external AI service is contacted automatically.
- The offline archive ships descriptive metadata, not mirror copies of external copyrighted legal or policy documents. Revisit publishers for current source texts.

## Zero-harm / anti-inversion

Public finance scrutiny should support lawful accountability and reduced harm, not doxxing, suspicion-by-association, targeting civilian economic infrastructure, coercion of individuals, or mislabeling legitimate defence aid as aggression finance. These controls reduce misinformation risk but do not guarantee misuse cannot occur.
